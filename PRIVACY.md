# Privacy Policy — CalcGrid

*Applies to the CalcGrid app for Mac (算格) and the `calcgrid` command line tool. Last updated: October 8, 2026.*

**CalcGrid does not collect any data.**

- There is no account and no sign-in.
- There is no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising code in the app or in the command line tool.
- The developer receives nothing about you, your Mac or your files.

## Your files

Your documents and Excel files stay where you put them: in the folders you choose on your Mac, and —
if you use it — in your own iCloud Drive. iCloud Drive is run by Apple under your Apple Account and
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); the developer of CalcGrid cannot read
what is stored there.

## Purchases

The app is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the purchase and does not share your
payment details with the developer.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at <https://github.com/sunfmin/calcgrid-cli/issues>.

# 隐私政策 — 算格（CalcGrid）

*适用于 Mac 上的算格 App 和 `calcgrid` 命令行工具。更新于 2026 年 10 月 8 日。*

**算格不收集任何数据。**

- 没有账号，不需要登录。
- App 和命令行工具里都没有统计、崩溃上报和广告的代码。
- 开发者收不到任何关于你、你的 Mac 或你的文件的信息。

你的文档和 Excel 文件只在你自己选的位置：这台 Mac 上你选的文件夹，以及你自己的 iCloud Drive（如果你用它）。
iCloud Drive 由 Apple 在你的 Apple 账户下提供，适用 Apple 的隐私政策；算格的开发者读不到里面的内容。

App 通过 Mac App Store 销售，购买由 Apple 处理，开发者拿不到你的付款信息。

对这份政策有疑问：在 <https://github.com/sunfmin/calcgrid-cli/issues> 提一个 issue。
